The seven installed-game fallbacks are the C5/C6 treasure maps. They live in
x64/dlcpacks/dlc_content_extra/dlc.rpf -> x64/textures/ui/ui_itemviewer.ytd.
Lexeditor resolves them on demand with games/rdr2/inventory_icons.py and stores
only generated PNGs in its private cache. No Rockstar texture is added here.

The 41 non-static raw values expand to 42 unique texture IDs because two book
records contain ordered alternatives. They are:
  UI_BOOK_FRANKHECK1
  UI_BOOK_GERMAN1
  UI_BOOK_JIMBOY1 UI_BOOK_JIMBOY2
  UI_BOOK_MADSCI
  UI_BOOK_OTISARB1
  UI_BOOK_OTISBLK1
  UI_BOOK_OTISBLK1 UI_BOOK_OTISBLK2
  UI_LETTER_MAYOR_PERM
  UI_MAP_SERIAL_KILLER
  UI_NOTE_DINO_01 through UI_NOTE_DINO_30
  UI_PHOTO_NORWEGIAN
  UI_PHOTO_SC_DAGUERROTYPE

These names are absent from all three shipped static UI_ITEMVIEWER atlas layers
(base textures_1, update_4 patch, and dlc_content_extra), and their JOAATs are
absent from the base atlas unresolved-hash list. Decompiled Story code confirms
that item UI data is a texture-hash / texture-dictionary / texture-type tuple;
doc_book also tries document texture handles as streamed TXDs for the physical
held prop. Therefore these are real document/item-database handles with no
static UI_ITEMVIEWER atlas PNG, not failed extractions. The editor's explicit
unavailable-artwork state is correct for them unless a future source proves a
specific renderable asset. Do not manufacture similarity-based aliases.
