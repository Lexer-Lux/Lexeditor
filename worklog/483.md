# #483 — Timed Blocks

## Requirement
A well-timed press as an enemy physical hit lands reduces that hit's damage
(proposal ×0.5). A visible cue shared with Universal Timed Hits. Success and
failure sounds, each chosen with a sound selector in the tweak's options
(Lexer, 2026-09-26). Off by default, and off is byte-identical to vanilla.

## Research (2026-09-26; see codex/ff8/timed-hits.md)
- Damage is applied at command resolution: `00494410` writes the target's
  new HP (`+0x18`, `004946BC`) before the enemy animation plays. The
  animation only displays the number later.
- So a block cannot reduce damage at the source. It has to refund half the
  damage at the hit frame and correct the displayed number.
- Input: R1 is bit `0x08`. Whether the ATB menu also consumes it during an
  enemy turn is still open.

## Next agent work
- Identify the hit-frame opcode, the moment damage shows. `0x91` and `0x9D`
  are the only opcodes that call into battle logic; neither is proved yet.
- Then design the refund and the cue, and implement it off by default.
