# Issue 498 — Separate SFX and Music volume sliders

## Requirements
Replace the single in-game Sound slider with separate SFX and Music sliders.
Each controls only its category; values persist across restart and stay
consistent with the active audio backend. Research menu + FFNx audio paths first.

## Findings (2026-09-22)
- FFNx.toml audio paths surveyed in the local install:
  - External SFX layer: `use_external_sfx = false`, `external_sfx_volume = -1`
    (auto), path `lexeditor-sfx`. Scales only external SFX files.
  - External music layer: `use_external_music = false`,
    `external_music_volume = -1`. Scales only external music files.
  - Both layers are disabled here; neither follows the vanilla menu slider.
- The vanilla menu Sound slider drives the game's internal audio path. No
  SFX/Music split point is known in the exe or codex (codex/ff8 has no audio
  knowledge). The `volume` byte in `plugins/ff8/init_data.py` is save config,
  not the menu slider.
- No implementation exists; nothing to remove or wire.

## 2026-09-26 in-game sliders built (branch claude/ff8-exe-patches)
- Lexer (2026-09-26): the ask is the in-game Config menu, not a Lexeditor
  slider. Music is one global value in `lexeditor-music-volume.dat`; the save's
  20-byte config block has no free byte.
- `plugins/ff8/music_volume_issue_498.py`, a Tweaks row labelled SFX AND
  MUSIC SLIDERS (`splitMusicVolume`, off by default):
  - The Config row table (`00B88970`, 9 rows + FFFF) is copied to
    `027AAD00` with a Music row (id 0x7E, type 0x21, offset marker 0xFFFE)
    after Sound. All 22 table references are repointed.
  - Nine config-byte accesses (`mov r8,[idx+01CFE738]`) are redirected through
    flag-preserving stubs, so the marker row uses the Music byte.
  - Writing it re-applies music and saves the file.
  - The music setter `0046C6F0` is scaled by Music/100, and the last request is
    kept for re-applying.
  - A text-lookup wrapper (`004BD630`, bank 2) supplies SFX, Music and their
    help lines.
- Evidence: 20 unicorn tests in tests/ff8/test_ff8_music_volume_issue_498.py.
  Every hook site is checked against the exe. An end-to-end save against the
  real exe writes the fragment when on and nothing when off.
- Unknowns for the game test: whether the Config window fits a 10th row (rows
  are 16 px), and whether FFNx external music bypasses `0046C6F0` when enabled.
- The earlier Lexeditor-side SFX/Music volume rows (FFNx external gains) are
  not what Lexer asked for. Whether to remove them is Lexer's call.
