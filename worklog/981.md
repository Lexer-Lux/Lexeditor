  green poison screen effect. This supersedes poison messages in Better UI.

  maximum flash duration, capped at that maximum.
- Forget Me Not: unchecked lootable containers white, tried locked red, searched
  empty/looted clear; real doors white before first interaction, locked red,
  successfully opened clear. Never expose illusory walls. Persist per save.

  restrictions, do not auto-open containers. Vanilla gold is not automatic.
- Better Sprint: adjustable stamina drain defaulting to actual vanilla rate,
  drain only while moving, cannot sprint at zero. Verify KF2 mechanics first.
- XP Bar: discreet thin bar flush across the absolute bottom, full screen width,
  showing progress toward next level.
