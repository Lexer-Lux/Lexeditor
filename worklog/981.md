# King's Field II / Verdite2

## User acceptance

"I want that in, added to Lexer's editor"; initially shared Mods and Settings.
Use a Lexer-owned fork of the existing Verdite2 port, not a new recompilation.
Japanese game numbering. Lexer explicitly authorized the fork for native controls.

Four independent user-library mods are requested. On the latest clarification,
controls are Settings, not a fifth player-facing mod:

- Controls settings: RT and RB attack, LT and LB magic, hold Circle run, Cross interact;
  keyboard E interact and hold Shift run. Preserve context-appropriate menu input.
  Replace Shift+E with F2 for Remaster Editor. Bindings and sensitivity belong in
  native settings, not a standalone Modern Controls mod. Check native support for
  independent run/interact and the hardcoded editor hotkey before choosing support code.
- Better UI: show the item name during its own pickup presentation; optional
  Poisoned / Poison cured notifications once per status transition.
- Hit Feedback: red enemy flash; duration is damage / max HP times the configured
  maximum flash duration, capped at that maximum.
- Forget Me Not: white on unsearched lootable containers; red after a locked attempt;
  none after empty search or taking the contents. Remember per-save exploration.
- Auto Pick Up All: walking over loose items AND gold collects them without Interact;
  preserve capacity, quest and lock restrictions and do not open containers automatically.
  Lexer corrected the premise: vanilla gold does not auto-collect either. No
  implementation may assume an existing gold auto-pickup routine.

Do not edit Verdite2's shipped sources or executables to implement gameplay mods.
Do not close/restart any game Lexer is using. Gameplay mods remain undeployed.
