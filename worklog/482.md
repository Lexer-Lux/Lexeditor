# #482 — Universal Timed Hits

## Requirement
A timed-hit bonus on every character's physical Attack. Squall keeps his
native trigger. A visible cue for everyone. Sounds for success and failure,
each chosen with a sound selector in the tweak's options (Lexer, 2026-09-26).
Off by default, and off is byte-identical to vanilla.

## Research (2026-09-26, done; see codex/ff8/timed-hits.md)
- The window is opened by animation opcode `0xAB`, which is only in gunblade
  sequences. The input is R1 (`0x08`), read by task `004BA6C0`. The bonus is
  quality ÷ 20, applied only by damage type 10 (`0048F480`), so a hit gets ×1.5.
- Other characters have no `0xAB` in their sequences and use damage type 1.

## Proposed design (for Lexer)
- Open the same trigger task for any party attacker's Attack, rather than
  inserting `0xAB` into every weapon's sequence.
- Apply a flat multiplier (proposal ×1.25) in the shared type-1 physical
  path when the stored quality is ≥ 20.
- Squall's type 10 path is unchanged, so his native trigger stays on top.
- Cue: the native trigger bar. Success/failure sounds are two sound
  selectors, played when the press is judged.

## Next agent work

## 2026-09-26 first hit-frame log, and v2
Lexer's first log (6,205 lines, 5 sequence owners, one `AB`) records the
animation stream but cannot identify the hit frame. Owners interleave frame by
frame: opcodes below 0x80 end an owner's frame. Nothing in that log marks when
damage lands, and `0x91`/`0x9D` never ran. The logger now also writes current
HP for participants 0-6 on every line (branch `claude/hit-frame-log-hp`).
Where a target's HP first drops, the instruction on that line is the hit
frame. If HP always drops before the attack animation, damage lands at the
command (as `codex/ff8/timed-hits.md` states) and the damage-number display
path is next.
Needs Lexer: after merge, one battle with the HIT-FRAME LOG tweak on
(several normal attacks by different characters, one Renzokuken, and one
enemy attack), then the new `lexeditor-hitframe.log`.

## 2026-09-27 second log (with HP)
The HP log shows damage landing mid-animation at frame boundaries, not at
the command (the codex said otherwise; corrected in `codex/ff8/timed-hits.md`).
Six drops: two enemy hits on party 0, a two-part spell on enemy 3, Squall's
gunblade hit on enemy 4 (the whole 363 at once), and one hit on party 1.
No single sequence opcode precedes every drop, because the hit is applied by
the action's queued battle task through `0048FE20`. Timed Hits and Blocks
should hook `0048FE20` rather than an opcode. Logger v3 (same branch) also
writes a line whenever `0048FE20` runs, to confirm that in game.
Needs Lexer: after merge, one more short battle with HIT-FRAME LOG on
(normal attacks, one enemy attack), then the log.

## 2026-09-27 Timed Hits built (branch claude/timed-hits)
Lexer's rule: one timing system for everyone, attack and defence, Squall
included. `plugins/ff8/timed_hits.py` (Hext, no driver):
- `004A8554`: records `timeGetTime` when R1 (bit 0x08 of the new-press word
  at [01D6D490]+0x12) is pressed. It sits after the three translator calls
  that Modern Controls hooks.
- `00491124` (inside 0048FE20, before the damage cap): if the last press is
  within the window, a party hit on an enemy is scaled by the bonus and an
  enemy hit on the party by the block percentage, with the success sound. A
  press up to 3 windows early plays the failure sound. One press, one hit.
- Squall's native trigger bonus is off: the 01D28D94 stores at 00485158 and
  004851D6 no longer write, so the gunblade factor stays 1 (vanilla and the
  melee-rework cave both read it).
- Sounds play through 0046B280(audio.fmt index); defaults 1 (menus) and 16
  (the menu refusal buzz, menu sound 5 via the table at 00B87D28).
- Tweaks row "TIMED HITS": toggle, window (50-600 ms), hit damage (100-300%),
  block damage taken (0-100%), success and too-early sounds chosen from the
  SFX list. The rendered row was checked headless.
Needs Lexer after merge: Timed Hits on; in battle press R1 as a hit lands
(attacks and enemy hits), early, and not at all; report the sounds, damage
and whether the window feels right.

## 2026-09-27 redesign: the press decides the hit (Lexer)
Input window W (ms). The attack's own hit chance (whichever formula is
active) sets the hit window = W x hit%, before the hit lands only; the crit
chance sets the crit window = hit window x crit%. Outside it, or no press:
miss. Implemented in `plugins/ff8/timed_hits.py` by replacing the game's
random byte at the physical hit/crit rolls (00492BA0, 00492B30, and
00492E10's three) and judging Squall's gunblade landing at 0048F530.
Fumbles sound at once; a late press arms the next hit (multi-hit attacks).
Blocks keep the old rule (press within W, damage taken x block %).
Removed: hit damage bonus, the short-lived perfect tier.
Not done: the time indicator (coloured triangle over the active character).
Needs Lexer after merge and push: Timed Hits on; Attack with several
characters pressing early, in the window, late and not at all; Squall
included; report hits, misses, crits and sounds.
