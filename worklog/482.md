# #482 — Universal Timed Hits

## Requirement
A timed-hit bonus on every character's physical Attack. Squall keeps his
native trigger. A visible cue for everyone. Sounds for success and failure,
each chosen with a sound selector in the tweak's options (Lexer, 2026-09-26).
Off by default, and off is byte-identical to vanilla.

## Research (2026-09-26, done; see codex/ff8/timed-hits.md)
- The window is opened by animation opcode `0xAB`, which is only in gunblade
  sequences. The input is R1 (`0x08`), read by task `004BA6C0`. The bonus is
  quality ÷ 20, applied only by damage type 10 (`0048F480`), so a hit gets ×1.5.
- Other characters have no `0xAB` in their sequences and use damage type 1.

## Proposed design (for Lexer)
- Open the same trigger task for any party attacker's Attack, rather than
  inserting `0xAB` into every weapon's sequence.
- Apply a flat multiplier (proposal ×1.25) in the shared type-1 physical
  path when the stored quality is ≥ 20.
- Squall's type 10 path is unchanged, so his native trigger stays on top.
- Cue: the native trigger bar. Success/failure sounds are two sound
  selectors, played when the press is judged.
