## Requirement

Casting a spell costs stamina on top of its attunement use. Per-spell costs
are copied from the later Souls games, never a flat rate. Vanilla behaviour
stays when the tweak is disabled. Every spell's stamina cost edits in the
editor.

## Design: a data mod, no native patch

DS1's Magic rows already carry a stamina field (whole number at row offset
14), and the engine subtracts it from the caster's stamina when the spell is
used (Souls Modding, param:magic, DS1 section). Vanilla leaves all 141 rows
at 0, which is why vanilla casts are free. Setting the field is therefore
sufficient: enabling the mod deploys costs, disabling it deploys vanilla's
zeros, and a native patch deducting the same field would charge twice. The
engine behaviour is documented, not re-derived; in-game acceptance confirms
it. Static probing found no contradicting evidence (SoloParamMan returns Magic
as parameter 14 with 48-byte rows; see the probes below).

explosion) stay 0. Row 14300 is Iron Flesh's NPC copy despite its reference
name. Elden Ring's pool matches DS1's scale, so values transfer verbatim.

## 0.2.0: no stamina recovery while casting (2026-10-13)

Lexer's playtest: costs deduct, but the bar refills during the cast, so they
barely matter. He wants Dark Souls III's freeze. Fixed as data, with no native
patch. Each of c0000 `a00.tae`'s 39 cast animations (those with a type-64
Cast magic event: 6400-6426, 6500-6526) gains a type-225 Stamina recovery
event at 0% spanning the whole animation. That is the event vanilla rolls
use, and the stamina update (RVA 0x31FCA3) scales recovery by
ActionCtrl+0x9C/100. DS3's 0.7 s post-animation delay is not reproduced,
because an event cannot outlast its animation. Built by the mod's
`tools/build_cast_recovery.py` (deterministic). The mod suite passes 10/10.
In-game: confirm stamina stays flat through a Soul Arrow cast and resumes
after it or after a roll-cancel. A mod that also edits `a00.tae` conflicts
at member level.

## Risks

  no-op (not a crash) is the failure mode.
- Heal-class costs are high (35-50), faithfully copied; they are editable
  per spell if playtesting wants them lower.
