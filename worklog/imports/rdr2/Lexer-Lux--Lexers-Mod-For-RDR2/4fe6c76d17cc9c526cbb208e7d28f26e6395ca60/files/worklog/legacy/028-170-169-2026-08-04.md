## #170 + #169 — 2026-08-04

Build `57B3F370A18476EB798DE39A81639BCEEA896A815FD0A1FA2EC9B96616B8B90D`.

#170 standing pop. The previous diagnosis (CLEAR_PED_TASKS before the exit clip)
was WRONG — removing it did not fix it. The log proves the dive getup is issued
and completes on schedule (2046 ms, `mech_weapons_core@base@dive@unarmed@getup/
dive_getup_fwd`), and the pop is still there. The real difference is the FLAGS:
the crouch exit uses `0x10000410` and does not pop; the standing exit used
`0x20002C10` and does. `0x20002C10` evidently does not take the whole skeleton on
the frame it is issued, so the ped renders its base standing pose until the clip
binds, then drops back to the ground for the getup and rises again — exactly the
three-part motion reported. Standing exit now uses `0x10000410` too.

#170 sliding on entry and after the binocular exit. Nothing pinned velocity
during Entering / ExitingCrouch / ExitingStanding / SettlingStanding — only the
Prone state itself cleared it. All four transition states now pin horizontal
velocity; the authored clips own the movement.

#170 gun stuck in hand. `beginProne` now holsters to WEAPON_UNARMED on entry
while `BlockWeaponActions` is set, since nothing prone can use a weapon anyway.

#169 releasing at random — Lexer's own hypothesis was right. The hand-plane
correction pushed the anchor outward by up to 0.22 m in a single frame, which is
LARGER than the 0.16 m surface standoff. One frame of hand penetration could
therefore shove the root far enough off the face that the next probe found no
surface, tripping `lost_surface` and dropping him mid-climb for no visible
reason. Correction is now capped at 0.06 m and multiplied by the frame smoothing
factor so it eases in. Also raised the surface-loss grace from 700 ms to 1400 ms:
on irregular geometry the probe legitimately misses for a few frames while the
fit re-settles.
