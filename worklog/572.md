# #572: Character graphs: the extrema, the equation and the line

[Live GitHub issue and comments](https://github.com/Lexer-Lux/Lexeditor/issues/572)

## Requirement

From Lexer's list on the FF8 character screens: the equation text is too
small, the centred graph title is too small, the line is a staircase on
slowly-growing stats, the min/max text does not use the accent colour and is
not placed properly ("not above the line but overlapping it" on the XP
curves), and the equation reads as a seesaw on curves such as Edea's and
Irvine's MAG.

## Implementation state

On master. Earlier work fixed the equation size (13px through
`--lex-curve-formula-size`) and the extrema accent rule, both covered by
`tests/shared/test_curve_rendering.py`.

This pass fixed the two defects that reproduce:

- The least and greatest values left the line straight up, so on a steep curve
  they drifted back onto it, worst where the curve climbs into the top corner
  of the plot - which is what an XP curve does. They now leave the line along
  the line's own normal, and the placement is measured against the samples that
  were actually drawn: each candidate distance and side is checked against the
  nearest point of the curve, and the first that clears by five units wins.
- Each term of the equation took the local slope with no bound. On a curve that
  climbs and comes back down the steepest term measured 83 degrees over from
  level - the seesaw. The tilt is bounded to 40 degrees, the bound the
  single-formula layout already used, so a term follows the curve until
  following it would cost the reader the text.

## Evidence

Measured in a headless browser on six curve shapes (two XP curves, MAG, flat,
a near-vertical climb and a parabola): no drawn sample falls inside the turned
number on any of them, and the steepest term on the parabola measured 40
degrees against 83 before. `tests/shared/test_curve_rendering.py` asserts both
rules, and `tests/ff8` (316 passed, 3 skipped) plus the two FF8 graph browser
checks pass. Screenshots: `%TEMP%/lexeditor-dev/atlas/xp-card.png`,
`arc-card.png`.
